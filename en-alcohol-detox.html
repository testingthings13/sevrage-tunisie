Alcohol withdrawal in Tunisia: a detox never attempted alone.

Every substance has its own withdrawal, and alcohol’s is one of the few that can become dangerous: stopped abruptly, without medical supervision, it exposes the patient to seizures and to delirium tremens. So stopping is never decided in advance and never alone — it is prepared, and it is supervised. At Clinique Espoir, in Tunis, alcohol detox takes place in hospital, under monitoring, with a protocol decided after examination.

The signs of withdrawal

  • Tremor, sweating, anxiety, nausea and insomnia, from 6 to 12 hours after the last drink.
  • Needing a drink in the morning for the day to start, or attempts to stop alone that have already failed, several times.

Two complications make this withdrawal serious — and justify the hospital:

  • Seizures possible within 48 hours of stopping.
  • Delirium tremens between 48 and 96 hours: confusion, hallucinations, fever.

One of these signs alone is enough to justify a call, and the call commits you to nothing. If life is in danger — a seizure, confusion, loss of consciousness — dial 190 (SAMU), then call us: we prepare for the arrival. To know what to do right now, three questions are enough to point the way.

The care at the clinic

Withdrawal is inpatient: benzodiazepines on tapering doses under monitoring, hydration, vitamin B1 to prevent neurological complications. The withdrawal syndrome is treated, not endured. This is what “alcohol rehab” usually means: the detox is the short part, the treatment is the stay. The stay follows the same path for everyone:

  • Admission assessment: clinical examination; blood, liver and heart work-up; screening for associated disorders — anxiety, depression, sleep, suicide risk.
  • Medically supervised withdrawal: tapering doses, vital signs checked several times a day, prevention of seizures and delirium.
  • Stabilisation: sleep falls back into place, mood lifts or reveals what it was hiding — this is where the disorder masked by alcohol is treated.
  • Work on craving, then the aftermath: motivational interviewing, cognitive behavioural therapy, a written plan for the tipping moments, and close follow-up consultations after discharge.

The detail is on the home page: the protocol, from the first day to the last, and the Alcohol entry among the other substances treated.

How long?

The acute phase lasts 5 to 7 days; fatigue and disturbed sleep persist for several weeks. A stay of two to four weeks additionally allows sleep to stabilise, the underlying disorder to be treated and the work on craving to begin; the duration is reviewed every week with the patient.

These durations are indicative: they vary with how long the drinking has gone on, the quantities, age and general health. The protocol is decided after examination.

For the family

It is often a relative who calls first, and you do not need the patient’s consent to ask for advice: a first call serves to describe the situation and gauge how urgent it is. Do not try to manage an alcohol stop at home in the patient’s place, and avoid the ultimatum you will not keep. If he or she refuses to hear about it, the page He refuses to come: what can you do? details what helps — and what closes the door.

The questions people don’t dare ask

Can alcohol withdrawal be done at home?

No, not without medical advice: stopping alcohol without supervision exposes the patient to seizures and to delirium tremens. The decision is made after examination, never in advance and never alone — that is precisely what the first call is there to assess.

Will it stay confidential?

Medical confidentiality covers the entire stay. No information is passed to an employer, an administration or any third party without the patient’s written consent.

What does it cost?

It depends on the type of care and the room. Prices are given on the phone, in detail and without obligation, before any admission.
