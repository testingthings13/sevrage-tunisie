What does it cost? What drives the price, and how to get it.

The clinic's prices are not published online — not to hide them, but because they depend on what the situation calls for. A detailed quote is given on the phone, without obligation, before any admission: you know exactly what to expect before deciding anything.

What makes the price vary

  • The type of care: an outpatient consultation, day hospital and a full inpatient stay do not cost the same — this is the first factor, and it follows the medical assessment, not a catalogue choice.
  • The room: single or double, for inpatient stays.
  • The length of stay: the acute phase of a withdrawal generally lasts a week; a stay of two to four weeks also stabilises sleep and treats the underlying disorder. The length is reviewed every week with the patient — the quote follows the same logic.

How to get a quote

Call +216 52 247 043 — a caregiver answers at any hour — or request an appointment to be called back during the day. Describe the situation: the substance or the disorder, since when, the general state of health. You leave with a detailed quote, item by item, and the call commits you to nothing. If you are calling from outside Tunisia, say so — the first assessment is done by phone, before you travel.

Insurance and coverage

If you have health insurance or another form of coverage, raise the question on the first call and bring your card on admission day — it is part of the documents to prepare before the stay.

The questions people don’t dare ask

Why are prices not published online?

Because they depend on what the situation calls for: the quote follows the assessment, not the other way round. A published price would be wrong for most situations — too high for some, too low for others.

Does a quote commit me to anything?

No. It is given on the phone, in detail and without obligation, before any admission. Asking for a quote is asking a question — not booking a bed.

What does the quote contain?

A breakdown of what is included, according to the care chosen — full inpatient stay, day hospital or consultation — and the type of room. You know what the amount covers before you decide.
