Coming from abroad: everything starts with a call, not a ticket.

The Espoir Clinic receives patients from Libya, Algeria, across North Africa and further afield — for medical withdrawal as for psychiatric care. Nothing is improvised at the airport: the assessment, the quote and the admission are prepared by phone, before the journey. A caregiver answers at any hour on +216 52 247 043.

Before the journey

  • The first call: describe the situation — the substance or the disorder, since when, the general state. It is what says whether to come, when, and in what setting: consultation, day hospital or inpatient stay.
  • The quote: given before departure, in detail and without obligation — you know what to expect.
  • The documents: passport, current prescriptions — in any language —, most recent tests. The full list is on the Preparing your stay page.

On arrival

The clinic is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week: an arrival is coordinated at any hour, and medical transport can be arranged on request. Because the admission was prepared before departure, it does not feel like a discovery: the team is expecting you, the setting is decided, and so is the room — single or double.

During and after the stay

Care takes place in English, French or Arabic, with nursing presence day and night. On discharge, if a doctor follows you in your country, we coordinate with them with your consent — they receive a report stating the diagnosis, the treatment and the proposed follow-up. You do not leave without a plan.

For families at a distance

It is often a relative back home who calls first — and you do not need the patient's consent to ask for advice. If they refuse to come, the page They refuse treatment: what can we do? details what helps, even from a distance.

The questions people don’t dare ask

In which languages does care take place?

In English, French and Arabic — from the first call and throughout the stay.

Do I need to come in person for the first assessment?

No. Assessment starts by phone, before the journey: the situation, the substance or the disorder, the general state, the degree of urgency. The quote is also given before departure — you do not travel towards the unknown.

How long should I plan to stay?

The acute phase of a withdrawal generally lasts a week; a stay of two to four weeks also stabilises sleep and treats the underlying disorder. The length is reviewed every week with the patient.

The information on this page is general and does not replace a medical consultation. Every situation is assessed by phone, before any decision to travel.
