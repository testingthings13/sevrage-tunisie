You are a doctor: referring a patient.

A direct line for colleagues, at any hour: +216 52 247 043. A psychiatrist calls back the same day.

What we take on

  • Medically supervised withdrawal: alcohol, benzodiazepines and hypnotics, opioids, cannabis, cocaine and stimulants, pregabalin and diverted medicines, tobacco.
  • Acute psychiatric decompensations requiring a protective admission.
  • Dual diagnosis: addiction with a psychiatric disorder.
  • Day hospital and outpatient consultations, as follow-on from an inpatient stay.

The referral letter

A brief letter is enough. The details that save us time:

  • Substance or substances, doses, how long, last use
  • History of withdrawal, seizures or delirium
  • Current treatment, including self-medication
  • Psychiatric history and assessment of suicide risk
  • Comorbidities: hepatic, cardiac, metabolic
  • The most recent laboratory results available
  • Family and social context, and who the contact person is

Send it to contact@psychiatrie-sevrage.com or hand it to the patient.

Timelines

  • Emergency: assessment the same day, with admission possible straight away.
  • Planned withdrawal: a preliminary consultation within a few days, then a date set with the patient.
  • Simple consultation: by appointment.

Reports back to you

You receive an admission report, then a discharge report setting out the protocol conducted, the diagnosis made, the discharge treatment and the proposed follow-up. The patient remains yours: we take over an episode, not the relationship.

Sending a document

To send test results or a report, write to contact@psychiatrie-sevrage.com. A secure upload space is in preparation; until then, avoid sending identifying documents through unencrypted messaging.
