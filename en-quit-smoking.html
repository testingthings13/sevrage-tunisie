Quitting smoking: the withdrawal is short, the habit is long.

Tobacco withdrawal does not endanger life — but it defeats more attempts than any other, because people believe it is simple. Physical withdrawal lasts two to four weeks; the gesture and the habit last longer, and that is where help changes everything. At the Espoir Clinic in Tunis, quitting smoking is prepared and accompanied. A caregiver answers at any hour on +216 52 247 043.

Signs of withdrawal

  • Irritability, difficulty concentrating, restlessness, increased appetite.
  • A craving that is very strong — but brief: a few minutes, not a few hours. Knowing this changes how you get through it.

The care at the clinic

  • Properly dosed nicotine replacement: patches, gum — failure most often comes from a dose that is too low, not from the replacement itself.
  • Trigger management: the coffee, the wheel, the break — each trigger is identified and prepared for, in advance and in writing.
  • Attention to psychiatric treatments: stopping smoking changes the effect of some medications — a point the clinic watches closely, and a reason not to quit “on your own” when you are followed in psychiatry.

How long?

Physical withdrawal lasts 2 to 4 weeks. The gesture and the habit last longer — which is why the follow-up does not stop when the withdrawal does. Relapse is part of the story of most addictions: coming back early changes everything, and no one here reproaches it.

And solvents — glue, thinners, gas?

Another inhaled product, an entirely different gravity: solvents — glues, thinners, petrol, gas — mostly affect adolescents, and families often hide the problem for a long time. Physical withdrawal is brief, but these products damage quickly: the clinic starts with a neurological, liver and kidney check-up, then the care is as much family and school work as it is medical. If this is your child's situation, call: the first call serves to describe it, and the They refuse treatment page helps when the dialogue is closed.

The questions people don’t dare ask

Do I need to be admitted to quit smoking?

No — tobacco alone is treated as an outpatient. The question changes when a stay is already planned for another substance or a disorder: quitting at the same time, or later, is then decided with the doctor, case by case.

Is nicotine replacement dangerous?

No — properly dosed, it delivers nicotine without the smoke, and it is the smoke that kills. The dose is adjusted in consultation: too low, and the craving persists; that is the most common mistake of solo attempts.

Why did my attempts alone fail?

Because physical withdrawal is only half the problem: the gesture, the schedule and the trigger situations outlast it. That is exactly what is worked on with a caregiver — not a question of willpower.
